# Campaign Planning

Use when a product, feature, event, or offer needs coordinated promotion.

## Inputs

- Objective
- Audience
- Offer
- Channels
- Constraints

## Workflow

1. Define the behavior the campaign should change.
2. Choose a narrative and channel roles.
3. Map required assets and owners.
4. Set leading indicators, guardrails, and review points.

## Output

- Campaign brief
- Channel plan
- Asset map
- Measurement plan

## Boundaries

- Do not promise unsupported results.
- Do not use every channel by default.
- Separate organic and paid assumptions.

Explicit user instructions take priority over this workflow. Ask for missing decision-critical input. Do not invent evidence, tool results, permissions, or completed actions.
