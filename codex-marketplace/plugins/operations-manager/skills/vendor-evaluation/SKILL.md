# Vendor Evaluation

Use when selecting or renewing a vendor without providing legal or financial advice.

## Inputs

- Business need
- Candidates
- Constraints
- Vendor evidence

## Workflow

1. Define must-have and weighted criteria.
2. Normalize vendor evidence and unknowns.
3. Compare risks, implementation effort, and fit.
4. Recommend next diligence steps and a provisional choice.

## Output

- Evaluation matrix
- Risk register
- Due-diligence questions
- Recommendation

## Boundaries

- Do not invent vendor claims.
- Do not provide legal approval.
- Expose missing evidence.

Explicit user instructions take priority over this workflow. Ask for missing decision-critical input. Do not invent evidence, tool results, permissions, or completed actions.
