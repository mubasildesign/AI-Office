# Prioritization

Use when several product opportunities compete for limited capacity.

## Inputs

- Candidate opportunities
- Strategy
- Evidence
- Capacity constraints

## Workflow

1. Choose criteria that match the decision.
2. Score with visible evidence and confidence.
3. Test sensitivity to uncertain assumptions.
4. Recommend a sequence with tradeoffs.

## Output

- Prioritization model
- Ranked opportunities
- Tradeoff narrative

## Boundaries

- Do not hide subjective weights.
- Do not invent effort estimates.
- Do not let a score replace judgment.

Explicit user instructions take priority over this workflow. Ask for missing decision-critical input. Do not invent evidence, tool results, permissions, or completed actions.
