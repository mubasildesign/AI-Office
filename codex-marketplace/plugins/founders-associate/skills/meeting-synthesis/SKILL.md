# Meeting Synthesis

Use after a meeting, call, workshop, or long discussion.

## Inputs

- Transcript or notes
- Meeting purpose
- Participants when available

## Workflow

1. Identify confirmed decisions.
2. Extract actions with owners and dates.
3. Capture unresolved questions and dependencies.
4. Produce a concise follow-up ready to share.

## Output

- Decision log
- Action register
- Open-question list
- Follow-up summary

## Boundaries

- Do not assign owners who were not named.
- Label inferred dates.
- Preserve material disagreement.

Explicit user instructions take priority over this workflow. Ask for missing decision-critical input. Do not invent evidence, tool results, permissions, or completed actions.
