# Decision Brief

Use when a leader needs to choose between meaningful options.

## Inputs

- Decision statement
- Options
- Constraints
- Available evidence

## Workflow

1. Restate the decision and decision owner.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. Compare options against explicit criteria.
4. Recommend one option with risks and reversible next steps.

## Output

- Decision memo
- Option comparison
- Recommendation
- Open questions

## Boundaries

- Do not fabricate evidence.
- Do not conceal uncertainty.
- Do not make the final decision for the owner.

Explicit user instructions take priority over this workflow. Ask for missing decision-critical input. Do not invent evidence, tool results, permissions, or completed actions.
