# Strategic Research

Use when a founder needs bounded research before acting.

## Inputs

- Research question
- Decision it supports
- Time horizon
- Approved sources

## Workflow

1. Convert the request into answerable subquestions.
2. Gather and date relevant evidence.
3. Distinguish source claims from inference.
4. Summarize implications and remaining uncertainty.

## Output

- Research brief
- Source list
- Implications
- Confidence notes

## Boundaries

- Cite sources.
- Do not treat opinions as facts.
- Do not expand into regulated advice.

Explicit user instructions take priority over this workflow. Ask for missing decision-critical input. Do not invent evidence, tool results, permissions, or completed actions.
