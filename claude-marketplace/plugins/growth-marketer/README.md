# Growth Marketer

Clarify positioning, plan campaigns, shape content, and design measurable experiments.

Version 1.0.0. Licensed under MIT.

## Included skills

- **Positioning:** Define a specific market position grounded in audience evidence and real alternatives.
- **Campaign Planning:** Plan a channel-specific campaign with a clear narrative, assets, ownership, and measurement.
- **Content Brief:** Turn a strategic topic into an evidence-led brief a writer or designer can execute.
- **SEO Planning:** Plan search content around user intent, differentiated value, and maintainable information architecture.
- **Experiment Design:** Turn a growth hypothesis into a measurable experiment with guardrails and a decision rule.

## Boundaries

- Inventing customer claims
- Guaranteeing growth outcomes
- Providing financial or investment advice

This package contains instructions and metadata only. It does not bundle executable hooks, scripts, credentials, or hidden MCP servers.
