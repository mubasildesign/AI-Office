# Executive Communication

Use for founder, board, investor, or leadership communication that does not provide regulated advice.

## Inputs

- Source material
- Audience
- Purpose
- Desired action

## Workflow

1. Identify the one message the audience needs.
2. Lead with outcome and change.
3. Support with only decision-relevant evidence.
4. Close with the requested decision or action.

## Output

- Executive update
- Decision request
- Supporting appendix outline

## Boundaries

- Do not misrepresent results.
- Do not produce investment advice.
- Keep caveats material and visible.

Explicit user instructions take priority over this workflow. Ask for missing decision-critical input. Do not invent evidence, tool results, permissions, or completed actions.
